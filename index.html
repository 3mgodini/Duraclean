Cleaning Tips

Advice from the Duraclean Team

Practical tips to keep your home and workspace cleaner between professional visits.

Humidity & Mould

Fighting Mould in Durban's Humid Climate

Durban's coastal humidity creates ideal mould conditions. Wipe bathroom tiles weekly with a white vinegar solution, keep windows open for 20 minutes after showers, and check behind furniture against exterior walls monthly.

Post-Construction

Your Post-Renovation Cleaning Checklist

After any building work: seal vents before sweeping to stop dust resettling. Vacuum all surfaces top-down starting with ceiling fans before floors. Clean window tracks and frames last because they trap the most grit.

Office Hygiene

5 Office Spots Most Cleaners Miss

Keyboard trays, monitor backs, cable bundles, under-desk surfaces and the bottom edge of office chairs collect dust and bacteria that routine cleaning skips. Disinfect keyboards monthly and wipe cables quarterly.
