Cleaning Tips

Advice from the Duraclean Team

Practical tips to keep your home and workspace cleaner between professional visits.

Humidity & Mould

Fighting Mould in Durban’s Humid Climate

Durban’s coastal humidity creates ideal mould conditions. Wipe bathroom tiles weekly with a white vinegar solution (1:1 with water), keep windows open for 20 minutes after showers, and check behind furniture against exterior walls monthly. Catch it early — mould spreads fast in KZN summers.

Post-Construction

Your Post-Renovation Cleaning Checklist

After any building work: seal vents before sweeping to stop dust resettling. Vacuum all surfaces top-down, starting with ceiling fans before you touch the floors. Wipe walls with a damp microfibre before any painting touch-ups. Clean window tracks and frames last because they trap the most grit. For heavy jobs, give us a call.

Office Hygiene

5 Office Spots Most Cleaners Miss

Keyboard trays, monitor backs, cable bundles, under-desk surfaces and the bottom edge of office chairs collect dust and bacteria that routine cleaning skips right past. Disinfect keyboards monthly and wipe down cables quarterly. A cleaner office genuinely reduces sick days. Workplaces with regular deep cleans see noticeably fewer illness-related absences, and your team feels the difference.
